Micro-lesson: Boundaries
- If someone says they’re okay, accept it and stay available.
- Validate feelings without exaggerating them.
- Keep your tone calm and grounded.
- Ask permission before problem-solving.
- Offer one small next step instead of a big plan.

Micro-lesson: Listening
- Keep your tone calm and grounded.
